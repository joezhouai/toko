# 常见问题 (FAQ)

## 关于 51Toko（拓客AI, TokoAI）

### 51Toko（拓客AI, TokoAI）是什么？

51Toko（拓客AI）是 AI 拓客服务——你的海关数据、B2B 平台、独立站照用，也汇进同一个 AI 底座，加工成可核实的对口买家报告。传统数据给你原料，真正难的是从一大堆里挖出「真对口」——这道活靠人体力填不满，我们替你在可靠数据上先挖出对口买家，最费力的那道我们做，你拿去判断和跟进。

### 51Toko 提供哪些服务？

三项获客服务：
1. **高质量线索与数据补全** — 以真实进口需求数据池为底座，规模化交付可触达的高质量线索；跨全网、多源级联把缺失的邮箱、电话、网站、行业、产品、采购记录等字段补全回来，补不了的如实标注。
2. **对口买家挖掘** — 你给产品、目标客户画像、竞对或标杆买家，我们从真实海关与采购数据里挖出产品级真对口买家（不是 HS 品类级的泛名单），交付「对口买家报告」——含匹配出的对口买家、匹配度与（美区）买家画像。
3. **意向客户研判** — 合规触达后自动回收打开 / 点击 / 回复等行为信号，按意向强度排序，越找越准。

## Frequently Asked Questions (English)

### What is 51Toko (拓客AI, TokoAI)?

51Toko (拓客AI, TokoAI) is an AI customer-acquisition service. Your customs data, B2B platforms, and standalone sites keep working as-is and also feed into one AI backbone, which turns them into a verifiable shortlist of right-fit buyers. Traditional data gives you the raw material; the hard part is digging out who is *truly* right-fit from a huge pile—that manual grind we do for you on reliable data, while you make the judgment and follow up.

### What services does 51Toko offer?

Three acquisition services:
1. **High-quality leads & data enrichment** — a real import-demand data pool as the base, delivering reachable high-quality leads at scale; missing emails, phones, websites, industries, products, and purchase records are enriched back across the web via multi-source cascading, with honest "not available" labels where data can't be filled.
2. **Right-fit buyer mining** — give us your product, target buyer profile, competitor, or benchmark buyer, and we mine product-level right-fit buyers (not HS-category generic lists) from real customs and procurement data, delivering a "Right-fit Buyer Report" with matched buyers, match scores, and (US) buyer profiles.
3. **Intent signal scoring** — after compliant outreach we collect open / click / reply signals and rank by intent strength, getting more accurate the more buyers it finds (self-evolving from real feedback).

### Who is 51Toko for?

- **Export manufacturers** — reach overseas buyers directly, skip middlemen
- **Trading companies** — expand the customer base, raise conversion
- **Cross-border e-commerce** — find distributor and retailer partners
- **Domestic B2B businesses** — any Chinese seller who needs to find customers

### How to use 51Toko?

1. Email toko@51toko.com with your product direction or target market—the more specific, the better
2. AI matches customers and generates a right-fit buyer report
3. We run compliant outreach and compile who opened / showed intent into a report
4. You review the report and follow up with interested prospects

No registration, no login—requests and results both live in email.

### What data sources does 51Toko use?

51Toko integrates multiple authoritative public sources:
- Customs import records (UK / EU / US / global customs)
- International trade-show exhibitor and buyer records (Canton Fair, CIFTIS, CSA-EXPO, etc.)
- Government procurement award data (EU / US / Australia, etc.)
- Public company information (company websites, etc.)

All data comes from legitimate public sources, with traceable origins, and we do not buy third-party lists; see the live data-pool page on 51toko.com for current coverage.
