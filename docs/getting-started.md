# Getting Started with 51Toko (拓客AI, TokoAI)

Welcome to 51Toko (拓客AI, TokoAI)! This guide helps you get started with AI-powered customer acquisition—tell us your product direction, and our AI matches customers, runs outreach, and tells you who's interested.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Describe your product direction

Send an email to toko@51toko.com describing what you export or trade and your target markets. The more specific, the more accurate and larger the result.

### 2. We match and reach

Our AI matches right-fit buyers from real trade records, runs compliant outreach, and collects open / click / reply / forward signals.

### 3. You review the report

You receive an intent-ranked report. Process judgment (whether AI's call holds up) and final judgment (who's worth pursuing) stay with you.

### 4. Follow up

You follow up with the interested buyers. Every round of real feedback makes the next round more accurate.

## 📚 What is 51Toko (拓客AI, TokoAI)?

51Toko (拓客AI, TokoAI) is an AI customer-acquisition service built on **AI + big data + self-evolution**:

- **Find right-fit buyers** from a 1.698M+ company pool (customs import records, global trade-show directories, EU public-procurement winners, public company info)
- **Match automatically** based on your product and target markets, at product level—not HS-category generic lists
- **Reach out** with AI-generated personalized, compliant emails
- **Track engagement** — see who opens, clicks, replies, and forwards
- **Learn and improve** — every round of real feedback makes the next round more accurate (越找越准)

We're not selling data or tools; data is the foundation, not the product. We start from the most basic data—public sources are collected, cleaned, mined, enriched, and verified into a verifiable company pool—then the three services form the full chain from data to intent: ① High-quality leads & data enrichment (data base) → ② Right-fit buyer mining (matching) → ③ Intent signal scoring (intent). Your existing data (customs data, B2B platforms, Shopify) keeps working and also feeds our AI backbone.

## 🎯 Three Services

### 1. High-quality leads & data enrichment

From a 1.698M+ company pool, screen reachable, right-fit customers (820K+ overseas, 225K+ domestic reachable; 61.5% high-quality, ~1.045M+ reachable). Or enrich incomplete leads across the web via multi-source cascading—email / phone / website / industry / product / purchase records—even from a single company name, with honest "not available" where data can't be filled.

### 2. Right-fit buyer mining

Give us a target list (even incomplete, or just a direction). Our buyer-matching engine does multi-dimensional cross-matching to surface truly right-fit buyers at product level, delivering a right-fit buyer report.

### 3. Intent signal scoring

On the right-fit buyer data, we run compliant cold outreach and auto-collect open / click / reply / forward signals, ranked by intent strength, delivering an intent-ranked report.
