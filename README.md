**TokoAI（拓客AI）** is an AI-powered customer acquisition system that helps export manufacturers and trading companies find overseas buyers automatically.

> **AI找客户，越用越准** - AI-powered customer acquisition, smarter with every use.

## 🚀 What is TokoAI?

TokoAI uses artificial intelligence to:
- **Match buyers automatically** from global customs data, trade show directories, and industry databases
- **Generate personalized outreach** with AI-crafted emails
- **Track engagement** - see who opens, clicks, and shows interest
- **Learn and improve** - the system gets smarter with every interaction

## 🎯 Who is it for?

- **Export manufacturers** looking for overseas buyers
- **Trading companies** seeking new importers
- **Cross-border e-commerce** businesses expanding globally
- **B2B companies** wanting to automate customer acquisition

Whether you're looking for **US buyers**, **European distributors**, or **Southeast Asian importers**, TokoAI matches automatically.

## 🔧 For Developers

See our [API examples](examples/) to integrate TokoAI into your workflow:

```python
# Quick example: Find buyers for solar panels
from toko import TokoClient

client = TokoClient(api_key="your-api-key")
buyers = client.find_buyers(
    product="solar panels",
    regions=["US", "Europe"],
    limit=100
)

print(f"Found {len(buyers)} potential buyers")
```
